# Third-party assets

## Sound packs (bundled under `keyclack/packs/`)

The recorded mechanical-switch sample packs shipped with keyclack come from
the [obsidian-click-clack](https://github.com/Acylation/obsidian-click-clack)
project by Chiyu Liang, released under the **MIT License**. They are genuine
recordings of real switches/boards (Cherry MX Blue/Brown/Red, ALPS-style, IBM,
and subtle-clicks), each with a small per-key-family set.

Files under `keyclack/packs/*/` are reproduced under the original MIT terms.
See https://github.com/Acylation/obsidian-click-clack for the source.

The pack format (a `manifest.json` mapping roles like `key`, `enter`, `space`,
`delete` to sample files) follows that project's convention.
